CALIFORNIA PTA LICENSURE

PTBC generally requires 30 continuing competency hours per standard renewal cycle, including 4 BLS hours and 2 ethics/laws hours. Eligible first-time renewals submitted on time require 15 total hours. Courses must meet California approval requirements; keep completion records for 5 years.
