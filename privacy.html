Privacy Policy

THE YOUNG CLUB is a Korean-speaking PTA and SPTA community welcoming people of every background. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

THE YOUNG CLUB은 국적과 배경에 관계없이 모든 PTA와 SPTA를 환영하는 한국어 중심 커뮤니티입니다. 이 개인정보 처리 방침은 수집 정보, 이용 목적과 선택권을 안내합니다.

Effective / Last updated: November 24, 2026

Our privacy practices in brief. We use account information, membership-review details, user-submitted content and basic visit statistics to operate The Young Club. For privacy questions or requests, email contact@theyoungclub.com. The retention and deletion procedures are still being formalized; this notice does not promise a fixed deletion timeline. 개인정보 관련 문의는 위 이메일로 보내 주세요. 데이터 보관 및 삭제 절차는 운영상 검토가 진행 중입니다.

1. Information we collect

  • Accounts: Your email address, authentication records and related account identifiers when you register or log in. Password handling is provided by the authentication service; we do not ask you to publish a password.
  • PTA and SPTA membership applications: Nickname, legal first and last name, PTA license state and number when applicable, student school and program details when applicable, enrollment-proof documents or images submitted by SPTAs, application statement, review status, and administrative decisions. Verification documents are intended for administrator review, not public display.
  • Content and interactions: Community posts, replies, reports, reactions such as likes, saved posts, moderation records and relevant dates or member identifiers. Blog guest comments may contain the name and message you submit without creating an account. Blog images and community uploads may be processed to display content you submit.
  • Private administrator chat: When you contact the administrator through the Blog, Community, PTA News or Jobs chat, we store your messages, replies, timestamps and source page so the conversation can continue after either party leaves. Signed-in conversations are associated with the member account. Visitors without an account may provide a display name and optional contact email; a random, private conversation key stored in that browser is used to reopen the chat. Chat contents are visible only to authorized administrators and the person who can access the matching member account or browser conversation key. Online indicators reflect recent chat activity; they are not a guarantee of an immediate response. Email is optional and does not trigger automated reply emails. Do not share patient, medical, financial or password information through chat.
  • Job postings: Job title, company, location, employment type, compensation, description, poster account information and application email or URL when supplied. Saved-job preferences and listing interactions may be associated with an account or browser.
  • Account bans and appeals: When a full administrator bans a member, we store the affected account identifier, the stated reason, the moderator identity and the date. A banned user who successfully verifies their account credentials may see their own reason in a private popup before the session is ended. Banned accounts cannot use member-only services but may contact the site as visitors through the public support chat. Administrators can permanently delete a banned Auth account to permit a new registration. Hard deletion may also remove associated membership, community, job and support records, while some operational audit entries may be retained in anonymized form.
  • Signed-in account activity and administrative directory: While a signed-in member actively uses the website, we record the account identifier and an approximate last-active time. Full administrators can review account names, nicknames, email addresses, registration and membership review dates, assigned staff roles, and whether the most recent activity was within approximately two and a half minutes. This indicator does not verify that someone is currently looking at the page. Activity before this feature was introduced is not retroactively available. Only full administrators can view the complete user directory or change administrator roles.
  • Visitor and content counts: Random browser identifiers stored in local storage and hashed identifiers used to estimate daily/total visits and individual article, community-topic and job-post views. We also aggregate daily visits by broad public site section (such as Home, Blog, Jobs and Shop) for an administrator-only dashboard, using the same hashed random browser identifier to avoid counting repeated visits on the same day and page. The dashboard displays totals rather than individual browsing histories. These counts are not a measure of unique human beings. Necessary technical metadata may also be processed by hosting and service providers.

회원 계정 이메일, 면허 인증 신청 정보, 게시글·댓글·신고, 채용공고 정보 및 방문·조회수 집계를 위한 브라우저 식별자를 처리합니다.

로그인한 회원의 최근 활동 시각을 기록하여 전체 관리자의 계정 관리 화면에 표시합니다. 온라인 표시는 약 2분 30초 내 활동을 기준으로 한 추정치이며 정확한 실시간 접속 여부를 뜻하지 않습니다. 전체 계정 이메일 및 관리자 권한 변경 기능은 전체 관리자에게만 제공됩니다.

관리자는 강퇴 사유·처리 시각·처리자 정보를 기록합니다. 강퇴 회원은 로그인 시 본인 사유를 확인한 뒤 로그아웃되며, 비회원 문의는 계속 가능합니다. 기존 계정을 영구 삭제하면 연관된 멤버십·게시물·댓글·문의 내역 등이 함께 삭제될 수 있습니다.

채팅 문의 시 메시지·답변·시각과 채팅방 식별 정보를 저장합니다. 비회원은 같은 브라우저의 대화 키로 이전 문의를 확인할 수 있습니다. 담당 관리자만 문의 내용을 확인할 수 있으며 자동 이메일 알림은 제공되지 않습니다. 환자정보·비밀번호 등 민감정보는 전송하지 마세요.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide account access, review PTA licenses and SPTA school enrollment for membership access, operate discussions and job postings, moderate misuse or reported content, maintain security and reliability, and display aggregated site, section and content view counts. We do not require visitors to sign up to read public blog content.

서비스 운영, PTA 인증, 콘텐츠 관리, 보안, 방문 및 조회수 통계에 사용합니다.

3. What other people can see

Public blog comments display the name and message submitted. Approved job listings may display the contact email or application link entered by the poster. PTA Community topics and replies are intended for approved members and administrators. Administrators review PTA license details, SPTA enrollment evidence, application information and content reports. Nicknames can be visible alongside member contributions; legal names and verification images are not intended to appear on public community pages. Do not share patient information or sensitive personal information in posts or comments.

4. Service providers and disclosures

The website currently relies on third-party infrastructure including Supabase for account/data services, Cloudflare for website delivery, and GitHub for source-code/deployment workflows. These providers may process technical information needed to operate their services under their own applicable terms and privacy policies. We may disclose information when legally required or reasonably necessary to protect users and the service. This notice does not claim that every provider collects the same categories of data.

사이트 운영을 위해 Supabase, Cloudflare 등 기술 서비스 제공업체를 이용합니다.

5. Storage, security and retention

Account, application and content records are stored in our backend services, and visitor-count identifiers are stored locally in browsers with related hashed values in site databases. The service uses access controls intended to limit access to private membership records, SPTA verification uploads and administrative actions. Authorized review personnel may need access to submitted documents to verify eligibility. No online system can guarantee perfect security. Retention periods and deletion workflows require further operator verification; we do not promise an automatic deletion period that has not been implemented.

6. Your choices and rights

You can choose what to post and avoid including sensitive information. Browsers generally allow you to clear locally stored site data, though doing so may cause a future visit to count as a new browser. Applicable law may give you rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal information. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate operations or legal obligations. You may send privacy-related requests to the contact email listed below. We may need to verify a request before taking action.

관련 법률에 따라 정보 열람·정정·삭제를 요청할 권리가 있을 수 있으며, 개인정보 관련 요청은 하단 이메일로 보내 주세요.

7. Children

The Young Club's professional membership and job-related features are not intended for children under 13. Please do not submit children's personal information to the website.

8. Updates to this policy

We may revise this policy when our services or information practices change. The latest effective date appears above. Material updates should be communicated through the website as appropriate.

9. Privacy questions and requests

Contact The Young Club

For privacy questions or requests relating to access, correction or deletion of information, email contact@theyoungclub.com. Please do not include passwords, full license documents or sensitive information in your initial email. We may request identity verification and respond based on applicable requirements and operational capabilities.

개인정보 문의 및 열람·정정·삭제 요청은 위 이메일로 보내 주세요. 최초 문의에는 비밀번호나 면허 증빙 자료 등 민감한 정보를 첨부하지 마세요.
