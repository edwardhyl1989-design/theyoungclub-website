1. Information we collect

  • Accounts: Your email address, authentication records and related account identifiers when you register or log in. Password handling is provided by the authentication service; we do not ask you to publish a password.
  • PTA membership applications: Display name, legal first and last name, license state and number, application statement, approval status and administrative review information.
  • Content and interactions: Community posts, replies, reports and their dates and author identifiers. Blog guest comments may contain the display name and comment text you voluntarily submit without an account.
  • Job postings: Job title, company, location, employment type, compensation and description, along with application email or URL when provided by the poster.
  • Visitor and content counts: Random browser identifiers stored in local storage and hashed identifiers used to estimate daily/total visits and individual article, community-topic and job-post views. These counts are not a measure of unique human beings. Necessary technical metadata may also be processed by hosting and service providers.

회원 계정 이메일, 면허 인증 신청 정보, 게시글·댓글·신고, 채용공고 정보 및 방문·조회수 집계를 위한 브라우저 식별자를 처리합니다.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide account access, review PTA eligibility, operate discussions and job postings, moderate misuse or reported content, maintain security and reliability, and display aggregated site and content view counts. We do not require visitors to sign up to read public blog content.

서비스 운영, PTA 인증, 콘텐츠 관리, 보안, 방문 및 조회수 통계에 사용합니다.

3. What other people can see

Public blog comments display the name and message submitted. Approved job listings may display the contact email or application link entered by the poster. PTA Community topics and replies are intended for approved members and administrators, while administrators can review membership application details and content reports. Do not share patient information or sensitive personal information in posts or comments.

4. Service providers and disclosures

The website currently relies on third-party infrastructure including Supabase for account/data services, Cloudflare for website delivery, and GitHub for source-code/deployment workflows. These providers may process technical information needed to operate their services under their own applicable terms and privacy policies. We may disclose information when legally required or reasonably necessary to protect users and the service. This notice does not claim that every provider collects the same categories of data.

사이트 운영을 위해 Supabase, Cloudflare 등 기술 서비스 제공업체를 이용합니다.

5. Storage, security and retention

Account, application and content records are stored in our backend services, and visitor-count identifiers are stored locally in browsers with related hashed values in site databases. The service uses access controls intended to limit private member records and administrative actions. No online system can guarantee perfect security. Retention periods and deletion workflows require further operator verification; we do not promise an automatic deletion period that has not been implemented.

6. Your choices and rights

You can choose what to post and avoid including sensitive information. Browsers generally allow you to clear locally stored site data, though doing so may cause a future visit to count as a new browser. Applicable law may give you rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal information. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate operations or legal obligations. We will provide an official request contact once the site operator confirms it.

관련 법률에 따라 정보 열람·정정·삭제를 요청할 권리가 있을 수 있으며, 요청 접수 연락처는 운영자 확인 후 게시할 예정입니다.

7. Children

The Young Club's professional membership and job-related features are not intended for children under 13. Please do not submit children's personal information to the website.

8. Updates to this policy

We may revise this policy when our services or information practices change. The latest effective date appears above. Material updates should be communicated through the website as appropriate.

We have intentionally not published an unverified email address. Before relying on this notice for ongoing operation, The Young Club will add a confirmed contact email or request channel for privacy inquiries and applicable rights requests.
