A Korean-Speaking PTA Community, Open to Everyone

THE YOUNG CLUB began to make U.S. Physical Therapist Assistant (PTA) education, licensing and career information easier to find and share in Korean. We connect PTAs and PTA students (SPTAs) through real experiences and a verified community. Rooted in Korean-speaking experiences, we warmly welcome PTAs and SPTAs of every background—Korean or not.
